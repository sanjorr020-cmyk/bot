# Short-Form Video

Produce ready-to-shoot short-form video content for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts: hooks that stop the scroll in the first 1-2 seconds, full scripts with shot direction and on-screen text, captions, and a sustainable posting system. The output should be shootable by one person with a phone unless the user says otherwise — every deliverable specifies what to say, what to show, and what text to overlay, second by second.

## When to use this skill

Use when the user wants:
- Scripts or concepts for TikTok, Reels, or Shorts (organic, not paid)
- Hook writing or hook fixing for vertical video
- A short-form content calendar or posting cadence
- To remix a competitor's or their own winning video
- Captions, on-screen text, and hashtag guidance for vertical video

Do NOT use for:
- Long-form YouTube videos (8+ min, chaptered) — use **youtube-scripts**
- Filming, editing, lighting, or gear questions — use **video-production**
- Paid vertical video ads (Spark Ads, Reels ads) — use **meta-ads** or **ad-copywriting** for the copy angle
- Overall channel/platform strategy and audience positioning — use **social-media-strategy**
- Turning blogs/podcasts into clips as part of a broader repurposing system — use **content-repurposing** (this skill can still script the individual clips)
- Webinar or live-stream scripting — use **webinar-scripts**

## Inputs to gather

Ask for these; assume the listed default when the user doesn't know:

1. **Platform(s)** — TikTok, Reels, Shorts, or all three. Default: script for TikTok-native pacing, note tweaks for Reels/Shorts.
2. **Goal** — reach/followers, engagement, or conversion (profile visits, link clicks, DMs). Default: reach.
3. **Niche and audience** — who watches and what they already believe. If undefined, pull from context or ask one sharp question; reference **customer-personas** if a persona doc exists.
4. **Who's on camera** — founder, employee, faceless voiceover, or UGC-style creator. Default: one on-camera person, phone camera, natural light.
5. **Production ceiling** — can they do jump cuts, b-roll, captions/editing app (CapCut is the safe assumption)? Default: talking head + text overlays + jump cuts only.
6. **Proof assets** — screenshots, results, before/afters, customer quotes. These make hooks specific.
7. **Existing winners** — links or descriptions of their top 3 videos by watch time or shares, if they've posted before. Critical for the remix workflow.
8. **Volume needed** — one script, a batch, or a calendar. Default: batch of 5 scripts on one content pillar.

## Process

1. **Lock the platform spec.** All three platforms: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920. Length targets: TikTok 15-34s for reach (up to 60s for tutorials), Reels 15-30s, Shorts up to 60s (up to 3 min allowed, but 30-45s is the retention sweet spot). Keep critical text inside the safe zone: roughly the middle 80% horizontally, and avoid the bottom ~25% (caption/UI overlap) and top ~10%.

2. **Pick ONE video goal and ONE idea per video.** A short can teach one thing, tell one story, or make one argument. If the user's idea contains "and," split it into two videos.

3. **Choose the format archetype.** Match to the idea and the user's production ceiling:
   - **Talking head** — direct-to-camera opinion, story, or tip. Lowest effort, highest trust. Best for founders and personal brands.
   - **Voiceover listicle** — VO over b-roll/screen recording, e.g., "5 tools that...". Faceless-friendly; each item needs its own visual.
   - **Skit / POV** — acted scenario ("POV: your client asks for the 4th revision"). Highest share rate in most niches; needs comedic timing.
   - **Tutorial / screen share** — show the actual doing. Highest save rate; keep to 3-5 steps max in under 60s.
   - **Greenscreen** — creator overlaid on a screenshot, article, chart, or competitor's post they react to. Great for commentary and newsjacking; the background image IS the hook.
   Decision rule: proof/how-to → tutorial; opinion/story → talking head; relatable pain → skit; list of resources → voiceover listicle; reacting to something visual → greenscreen.

4. **Write the hook first — spend 40% of total effort here.** The first 1-2 seconds decide everything: viewers who make it past 3 seconds are far more likely to finish. Write 5-10 hook candidates per video, pick the strongest. A complete hook has three layers that must all land in second one:
   - **Spoken line** — first words out of the mouth. Never "Hey guys" or "In this video." Start mid-thought.
   - **Visual** — something already moving or unusual in frame (walking, pointing at something, mid-action, a prop).
   - **Text overlay** — a headline restating or twisting the spoken hook, max ~8 words, on screen at 0:00.
   Test each candidate: would a stranger who's never heard of this account keep watching? If the hook only works for existing followers, rewrite it.

5. **Script the body with an open loop.** Structure: Hook (0-2s) → Context/stakes in one line (2-5s) → Payoff delivered in beats (5s-end) → Close. Rules:
   - Write at spoken pace: ~2.3 words/second. A 30-second video is ~65-75 words. Count them.
   - Cut every sentence that doesn't earn the next second. No intros, no "so basically," no recaps.
   - Change something visually every 2-4 seconds: jump cut, zoom punch-in, b-roll, new text overlay. Mark each change in the script.
   - Open a loop early ("the third one got me fired") and close it at the end to pull viewers to full completion — completion rate is the strongest ranking signal on all three platforms.

6. **Script the close by goal.** Reach goal: end abruptly on the payoff or a loopable line (video restarting seamlessly boosts rewatches — rewatches count as completion). Engagement goal: end with a one-line question that has a low-effort answer ("Which one are you, 1 or 2?"). Conversion goal: one CTA only, spoken + overlaid, max 5 words ("Link in bio for the template"). Never stack CTAs.

7. **Write the caption and hashtags.** Caption's job: add context the video didn't say, or bait a specific comment — not summarize. First ~125 characters show before truncation on Reels/TikTok; front-load. 3-5 hashtags: 1-2 niche-specific, 1-2 topical, at most 1 broad. Skip #fyp/#viral — they do nothing. Keywords in the spoken audio, on-screen text, and caption all feed search (TikTok and Shorts are search engines now).

8. **Set posting cadence.** Sustainable minimums that the algorithm rewards: TikTok 4-7/week (daily if possible — TikTok tolerates volume and misses), Reels 3-5/week, Shorts 3-5/week. Better to hold 4/week for 90 days than 2/day for two weeks. Post when the audience is on: generally 6-10am and 7-11pm local to the audience, but tell the user to check their own analytics after ~20 posts. Batch-shoot: one filming session should yield 4-6 videos. If the user needs a full multi-platform calendar, hand off to **social-content-calendar** with the scripts as inputs.

9. **Define the review-and-remix loop.** After each 10-15 posts, rank by: completion rate (benchmark: ≥60% for <15s videos, ≥40% for 30s+ is strong), shares and saves per 1,000 views (≥5 is a winner signal), and follows per view. For any video that beats the account average by 2x+ on any of these, create 2-3 remixes:
   - **Same hook, new payoff** — the hook proved itself; feed it different content.
   - **Same format, new topic** — the structure worked; swap the subject.
   - **Sequel/part 2** — reply to a top comment with a follow-up video (reply-with-video on TikTok is a built-in distribution boost).
   Repost near-identical winners after 6-8 weeks; most of the new audience never saw the original.

10. **Deliver.** Output scripts in the format below, plus the hook bank and cadence plan if requested. Flag anything that requires assets the user hasn't provided.

## Frameworks & templates

### Hook formulas (with worked examples)

| Formula | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Negative call-out | "Stop doing X" / "You're doing X wrong" | "Stop sending cold emails at 9am." |
| Specific result | Number + timeframe + outcome | "This 40-second change added $3,200/mo." |
| Contrarian take | "Unpopular opinion:" / "[Common advice] is a lie" | "Posting daily is killing your account." |
| Curiosity gap | Withhold the noun | "Nobody talks about the real reason clients ghost." |
| Direct call-out | "If you're a [niche], watch this" | "If you run a service business, don't skip this." |
| Stakes/loss | What it cost you | "This mistake cost me my biggest client." |
| POV/relatable | "POV: [painfully specific moment]" | "POV: the client says 'just one small tweak.'" |
| Mid-story | Start at the climax | "So the invoice bounces, and he's already on a plane." |

### Script template — talking head (30s)

```
TITLE: [working title / content pillar]
PLATFORM: [TikTok / Reels / Shorts]  LENGTH: ~30s  GOAL: [reach/engagement/conversion]

[0:00-0:02] HOOK
  SAY: "[hook line — start mid-thought]"
  SHOW: [mid-action: walking toward camera / pointing / holding prop]
  TEXT: "[≤8-word headline]"  (on at 0:00)

[0:02-0:05] STAKES
  SAY: "[one line: why this matters / open the loop]"
  SHOW: [jump cut, punch-in ~120%]
  TEXT: [keep headline or swap to stake line]

[0:05-0:24] PAYOFF — 3 beats
  Beat 1 SAY: "..."  SHOW: [cut/b-roll]  TEXT: "1. [3-5 words]"
  Beat 2 SAY: "..."  SHOW: [cut/b-roll]  TEXT: "2. [3-5 words]"
  Beat 3 SAY: "..."  SHOW: [cut/b-roll]  TEXT: "3. [3-5 words]"  ← close the loop here

[0:24-0:30] CLOSE
  SAY: "[abrupt payoff end / question / single CTA]"
  TEXT: "[CTA or question, ≤5 words]"

CAPTION: [1-2 lines, hook in first 125 chars, comment-bait or added context]
HASHTAGS: #[niche] #[niche2] #[topic] (3-5 total)
AUDIO: [trending sound at low volume under VO, or original audio]
```

### Worked example — voiceover listicle (B2B SaaS, faceless)

```
TITLE: 3 free tools that replace a $500/mo stack
PLATFORM: TikTok + Shorts  LENGTH: ~28s  GOAL: reach + saves

[0:00-0:02] HOOK
  VO: "These three free tools replaced five hundred dollars a month of software."
  SHOW: screen recording scrolling a bloated SaaS invoice
  TEXT: "3 free tools = $500/mo saved"

[0:02-0:10] TOOL 1
  VO: "First: [tool]. It does [job] — most people pay [competitor] forty bucks for this."
  SHOW: 5s screen capture of the tool doing the job (sped up 1.5x)
  TEXT: "1. [Tool] — replaces [competitor]"

[0:10-0:18] TOOL 2
  VO: "Second: [tool]. [One-line what it does]. The free tier is genuinely enough."
  SHOW: screen capture, cursor lands on the key feature
  TEXT: "2. [Tool] — free tier"

[0:18-0:26] TOOL 3
  VO: "Third one's the best — [tool]. [What it does]. Nobody believes it's free."
  SHOW: the most visually impressive output first, then the tool
  TEXT: "3. [Tool] 🤯"

[0:26-0:28] CLOSE
  VO: "Save this before you pay another invoice."
  TEXT: "Save this 📌"

CAPTION: "The full list with links is pinned in the comments. Which one are you trying first?"
HASHTAGS: #saas #smallbusinesstools #productivity
```

### Batch ideation matrix

To generate a batch fast, cross 4-6 content pillars against 5 angles; each cell is a video:

| Pillar ↓ / Angle → | Mistake to avoid | How-to | Hot take | Story/result | Myth vs. reality |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Pillar 1, e.g., pricing] | video | video | video | video | video |
| [Pillar 2, e.g., client comms] | video | video | video | video | video |

A 4x5 matrix yields 20 concepts; script the 5 with the strongest hook candidates first.

### Remix worksheet (for a proven winner)

```
ORIGINAL: [link/description] — [views] views, [completion %], [saves+shares per 1K]
WHY IT WORKED (pick one primary): hook | format | topic | emotion
REMIX 1 (same hook, new payoff): "[hook]" → new content: ...
REMIX 2 (same format, new topic): structure kept, subject swapped to ...
REMIX 3 (sequel): reply to top comment "[comment]" with ...
SCHEDULE: remixes spaced ≥5 days apart; repost original after 6-8 weeks
```

## Output format

Deliver as markdown with these sections (omit those not requested):

1. **Concept summary** — platform, length, archetype, goal, one-line premise (per video)
2. **Hook bank** — 5-10 candidates per video with the recommended pick marked and one line on why
3. **Script(s)** — timestamped table/block per the template: SAY / SHOW / TEXT columns for every beat, word count noted against the length target
4. **Caption + hashtags** — per video, first 125 characters flagged
5. **Shooting notes** — shot list grouped for batch filming (all talking-head takes together, all b-roll together), props/assets needed
6. **Cadence plan** (if requested) — posts per week per platform, batching schedule, posting windows
7. **Measurement + remix triggers** — the 2-3 metrics to check, the thresholds that trigger a remix, and when to review

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Hook lands in the first 2 seconds across all three layers: spoken, visual, and text overlay
- [ ] First spoken words are not a greeting, intro, or channel mention
- [ ] Text overlay at 0:00 is 8 words or fewer
- [ ] Script word count fits the length target at ~2.3 words/second (30s ≈ 65-75 words)
- [ ] One idea per video — no "and also" second topics
- [ ] A visual change (cut, zoom, b-roll, new overlay) occurs at least every 4 seconds
- [ ] An open loop is created in the first 5 seconds and closed near the end
- [ ] Exactly one CTA (or none, for pure reach plays) — spoken and overlaid, ≤5 words
- [ ] Caption adds context or baits comments rather than summarizing; hook within first 125 characters
- [ ] 3-5 hashtags, niche-weighted, no #fyp/#viral filler
- [ ] All on-screen text sits inside the safe zone (clear of bottom ~25% and top ~10%)
- [ ] Every SHOW direction is shootable with the user's stated production ceiling
- [ ] Cadence plan is sustainable for 90 days, not a two-week sprint
- [ ] Remix thresholds are defined with numbers (e.g., 2x account-average saves/1K views)

## Common mistakes

- **Warming up before the hook.** "Hey everyone, so today I want to talk about..." loses 30-50% of viewers before the content starts. Start mid-thought; context comes after the hook, if at all.
- **Writing at reading pace, not speaking pace.** A "quick 30-second script" that's 120 words forces rushed delivery or a 50-second video. Count words against the 2.3 words/second budget.
- **Hooks that only work for followers.** "Following up on yesterday's video..." means nothing on the For You page, where most reach comes from strangers.
- **Landscape thinking in a vertical frame.** Wide shots, small text, and content in the bottom quarter get covered by captions and UI. Frame face/subject in the upper two-thirds.
- **Treating all three platforms as identical.** Reposting TikToks with the watermark suppresses Reels reach; Shorts viewers tolerate slightly slower pacing and more tutorial depth; TikTok punishes slow starts hardest. Export clean and adjust the first 2 seconds per platform.
- **CTA stacking.** "Like, comment, share, follow, and check the link" reads as five asks and converts as zero. One ask, matched to the video's single goal.
- **Chasing trends outside the niche.** A trending dance on a B2B account buys views from people who will never convert and teaches the algorithm to show the content to the wrong audience.
- **Judging videos at 24 hours and deleting "flops."** TikTok and Shorts routinely re-surface videos days or weeks later; deleting resets the signal. Leave videos up unless they're off-brand.
- **Remixing the wrong signal.** Remixing the highest-view video instead of the highest completion/saves-per-1K video copies luck instead of copying what worked.
- **Text overlays that transcribe instead of headline.** Overlays should compress or twist the spoken line, not caption it verbatim — auto-captions already handle verbatim.
- **Quitting at 15 posts.** Short-form accounts commonly need 30-60 posts before the algorithm finds the audience; the cadence plan should assume a 90-day runway before strategy changes.
