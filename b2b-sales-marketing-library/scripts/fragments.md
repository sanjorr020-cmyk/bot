> ### SPIN question flow (Rackham)
> 
> - **Situation** (max 2–3; research should answer most): "How is your team handling [process] today?" "How many people touch that?"
> - **Problem**: "What breaks first when volume spikes?" "Where does that process slow down?"
> - **Implication** (the money layer — spend the most time here): "When that report is late, what happens downstream?" "What's that costing you per month in [hours/dollars/churn]?"
> - **Need-payoff** (prospect states the value themselves): "If you got those 10 hours a week back, what would the team do with them?" "How would fixing this change your Q4 target?"
>

> 5. **Set the spacing.** Early touches: 2–3 business days apart. After touch 4: widen to 5–7 business days. Ghosted sequences: 4–7 days between touches. Send Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am or 1–3pm recipient's local time as a default; avoid Monday morning and Friday afternoon. > ## The 3-Part Opener 
> Never start with "How are you doing today?" (Fake rapport) or "Is now a bad time?" (They will say yes).
> 
> ### The Pattern-Interrupt Opener
> 1. **The Name Drop:** "Hey [Name], it's [Your Name] from [Company]."
> 2. **The Permission (Slightly self-deprecating):** "I know I caught you completely out of the blue. You can absolutely hang up on me, but can I get 30 seconds to tell you why I called, and then you can decide if we keep going?"
> 3. **The Premise (If they say "Sure"):** "Usually when I talk to [Their Persona], they are pulling their hair out over [Top Pain Point]. Is that something you guys are actively trying to fix right now, or are you actually happy with [Status Quo]?"
