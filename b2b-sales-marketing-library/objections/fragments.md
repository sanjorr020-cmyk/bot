# Фрагменты: objections/

## LAER: базовая структура ответа на возражение

- Источник: Business Skills (astDeniss/business-skills), `objection-handling/SKILL.md`, строки 75–82
- Установлено в: `.claude/skills/objection-handling/SKILL.md`

> ### LAER (default structure for every response)
> 
> - **Listen** — let the prospect finish completely; note the exact words.
> - **Acknowledge** — one sentence, maximum two, validating the concern without agreeing it is a dealbreaker ("That's a fair question — most teams your size ask it").
> - **Explore** — ask the clarifying question to find the underlying cause and isolate ("Help me understand — is it the total price, or the fit within this year's budget?").
> - **Respond** — reframe with a matched proof point, under 30 seconds, ending in a redirect question.
> 
> The most common failure is skipping Explore: answering the stated objection instead of the real one.

## Таблица быстрых ответов на возражения

- Источник: Marketing Skills (coreyhaines31/marketingskills), `skills/sales-enablement/references/objection-library.md`, строки 9–16
- Установлено в: `.claude/skills/sales-enablement/references/objection-library.md`

> | Objection | Response (1-line) | Proof Point |
> |-----------|--------------------|-------------|
> | "Too expensive" | "Compared to what? Let's look at what the problem costs you today." | ROI case study showing payback in X months |
> | "No budget" | "When budget opens up, what would need to be true for this to be a priority?" | Customer who started with a pilot to prove value |
> | "Competitor is cheaper" | "They are — here's what you give up at that price point." | Feature comparison + customer who switched |
> | "Not the right time" | "What changes next quarter that makes it better timing?" | Cost-of-delay calculation |
> | "Maybe next quarter" | "Happy to reconnect. What would a pilot look like before then?" | Customer who started small and expanded |
> | "We use X already" | "How's that working for [specific pain area]?" | Customer who switched from X |

## Возражения «уже используем конкурента» и «пришлите письмо» (SDR cold call)

- Источник: SDR Operations Playbook (forma-norden/sdr-operations-playbook), `.agents/skills/sdr-cold-call-scripts.md`, строки 16–22
- Установлено в: `.claude/skills/sdr-operations-playbook/sdr-cold-call-scripts.md`

> ### Objection 1: "We already use [Competitor]"
> - **Bad Response:** "We are better/cheaper."
> - **Good Response (The Judo Move):** "I figured you did, [Competitor] is the 800lb gorilla in the space. Most of our best customers started on [Competitor]. Typically, they liked X about it, but got incredibly frustrated with Y as they scaled. Are you guys hitting that Y bottleneck yet?"
> 
> ### Objection 2: "Just send me an email"
> - **Bad Response:** "Okay, what's your email?" (You will never hear from them again).
> - **Good Response:** "Happy to, but to make sure I don't send you generic marketing fluff, what specifically should I focus on? The [Feature A] use-case, or the [Feature B] use-case?" (Keeps them talking).
